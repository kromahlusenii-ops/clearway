## 1. PRODUCT CONTEXT

### What This Is
The Clearway Accounts page is the core banking dashboard — the first thing users see after logging in. It's the Glass Ledger brought to life. Every element on this screen reinforces Clearway's core promise: you can see exactly where your money is, what it's doing, and what you're being charged.

### What Makes It Different From Every Other Banking App
Most banking apps show your balance and transactions. Clearway shows you:
- **Where your deposits are invested** (not hidden behind the scenes)
- **What Clearway earned from your money** (radical fee transparency)
- **How your money is allocated** in real time (bonds, infrastructure, cash)
- **A transparency score** that grades how visible your money's journey is

This is the "private banker in your pocket" that Tender promised but never delivered — except this one actually shows you the math.

### Design Philosophy
Adapted from the Paywave Mobile Apps aesthetic:
- Clean, card-based layout with generous whitespace
- Balance as the hero element — large, confident, centered
- Quick actions as an icon row for common tasks
- Transaction feed as a scrollable list with smart categorization
- Soft shadows, rounded corners, no visual clutter
- Mobile-first (375px base) that scales gracefully to desktop (1200px+)
